What it does

Everything a trading business actually needs

Not a general-purpose accounting package with a hundred settings. The parts a distributor, wholesaler or kiryana store uses every day.

Sales & purchase invoices

Pick a customer, pick a product, type the quantity. The price fills itself in and you overtype it when you have negotiated. Posting is one action, and it either records everything or nothing.

Stock that explains itself

Stock is not a number somebody edited — it is the sum of every movement. When a figure looks wrong you can see exactly which document caused it.

Customer & supplier ledgers

A running statement for every party, with a closing balance you can read down the phone. What the customer sees and what your receivables report says are the same number.

Reports that tie out

Sales by product, customer or date. Purchases, stock, receivables, payables. Every figure is derived from the same ledger, so two reports cannot disagree.

Print or export anything

Every list and report goes to PDF, Excel, CSV or the printer. Excel gets real numbers you can total — not text that looks like numbers. Print a month of invoices as one job.

Nothing is quietly deleted

Customers are deactivated, not removed. A wrong invoice is cancelled and reversed, and both the original and the reversal stay on record. Your history is always explainable.

On paper

Documents you can hand to a customer

  • Your business name on every invoice, statement and report.
  • Figures that line up. Money columns use tabular numerals, so decimal points sit in a straight line down the page.
  • Negatives in brackets — the accounting convention, which survives photocopying and black-and-white printing where red ink does not.
  • Headings repeat on every page, because a long table without them is unreadable by page three.

Getting going

Running in about ten minutes

Install it

One file, double-clicked. No Python, no database server, no administrator password. It installs for your user only.

Create your login

Ledgerly ships with no default password, so the first thing it asks is what yours should be.

Add what you sell

Your customers, suppliers and products. Opening stock goes in as a purchase or an adjustment.

Start invoicing

Post your first sale. Stock, balances, the ledger and every report update in the same instant.

Your data

It lives on your computer

Ledgerly keeps everything in one file under your own user folder. No server, no cloud account, nothing to lose access to if a subscription lapses or a company changes its terms.

That cuts both ways, so it is worth being plain about it: backing up is your responsibility. Copy that file somewhere safe — a USB stick, a network drive, or a synced folder — on a schedule you will actually keep to.

Where your file lives, and how to back it up

Uninstalling never removes your books

The database sits outside the installation folder on purpose. Removing or reinstalling the application cannot take your records with it — the uninstaller tells you where they remain.

Nothing is transmitted

The only time Ledgerly uses the internet is to check a licence key, once, when you activate it. After that it runs entirely offline, and a renewal is picked up quietly next time you are connected.
